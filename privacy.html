Zametki privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What the app stores

Your notes and attachments are stored on your own devices. The developer of Zametki runs no server and receives none of your data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, Zametki uses the drive.appdata permission to read and write files in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. It uses this folder only to sync notes between your devices. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Files are encrypted on your device before upload with a key that only your paired devices hold, so Google stores them without being able to read them.

Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain and the Android Keystore. They are not sent anywhere except to Google.

Voice notes

If you choose to have a voice note transcribed, the recording is sent to the Google Gemini API using an API key that you provide. This happens only when you ask for it.

Sharing

Zametki does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note removes it from your devices and from the synced copy. To remove everything the app keeps in Google Drive, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, and delete the hidden app data for Zametki. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, in the list of third-party connections.
