Zametki

Zametki is a personal notes app for macOS and Android. Notes are stored on your own devices and synced directly between them.

Zametki has no server of its own. When your devices are not near each other, they exchange encrypted changes through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks for one Google permission only, drive.appdata, which gives it access to that folder and to nothing else in your Drive.

Zametki is a private project and is not distributed through any app store.
